REFERENCE: Bg. 7.9

VERSE: puṇyo gandhaḥ pṛthivyāṁ catejaś cāsmi vibhāvasaujīvanaṁ sarva-bhūteṣutapaś cāsmi tapasviṣu

TRANSLATION: I am the original fragrance of the earth, and I am the heat in fire. I am the life of all that lives, and I am the penances of all ascetics.

PURPORT BY SRILA PRABHUPADA: Puṇya means that which is not decomposed; puṇya is original. Everything in the material world has a certain flavor or fragrance, as the flavor and fragrance in a flower, or in the earth, in water, in fire, in air, etc. The uncontaminated flavor, the original flavor, which permeates everything, is Kṛṣṇa. Similarly, everything has a particular original taste, and this taste can be changed by the mixture of chemicals. So everything original has some smell, some fragrance and some taste. Vibhāvasu means fire. Without fire we cannot run factories, we cannot cook, etc., and that fire is Kṛṣṇa. The heat in the fire is Kṛṣṇa. According to Vedic medicine, indigestion is due to a low temperature in the belly. So even for digestion fire is needed. In Kṛṣṇa consciousness we become aware that earth, water, fire, air and every active principle, all chemicals and all material elements are due to Kṛṣṇa. The duration of man’s life is also due to Kṛṣṇa. Therefore by the grace of Kṛṣṇa, man can prolong his life or diminish it. So Kṛṣṇa consciousness is active in every sphere.

Hare Krishna, dear devotees! 

✨ The All-Pervading Presence of the Lord ✨

In the Bhagavad Gita, Lord Krishna reveals the mysteries of His divine presence in the universe. In Chapter 7, Verse 9, He declares His all-encompassing nature, which is the foundation of Krishna consciousness.

🕉 The Fragrance of the Earth 🕉

"puṇyo gandhaḥ pṛthivyāṁ" [Bg. 7.9](https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/7/9/). The Lord says, "I am the original fragrance of the earth." This statement is not just a poetic expression; it is a fact. The sweet fragrance that we experience in nature is a manifestation of Krishna's presence. He is the essence of the earth, and His fragrance permeates every aspect of creation.

✨ The Fire of Life ✨

"catejaś cāsmi vibhāvasau" [Bg. 7.9](https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/7/9/). Krishna further reveals, "I am the heat in fire." Fire is a symbol of transformation, and Krishna is the spark that ignites the fire of life within every living being. His presence is the driving force behind all transformations, from the simplest to the most complex.

✨ The Life Force ✨

"jīvanaṁ sarva-bhūteṣu" [Bg. 7.9](https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/7/9/). The Lord declares, "I am the life of all living beings." Krishna is not just a distant creator; He is the very essence of life itself. His presence is what sustains and nourishes every living being, from the smallest microbe to the mightiest creature.

✨ The Penances of the Ascetics ✨

"tapasviṣu" [Bg. 7.9](https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/7/9/). Krishna says, "I am the penances of all ascetics." The Lord is not just a passive observer; He is actively involved in the spiritual pursuits of every sincere seeker. His presence is what inspires and guides them on their journey towards spiritual growth.

✨ The Active Presence of Krishna Consciousness ✨

"So Kṛṣṇa consciousness is active in every sphere" (Purport by Srila Prabhupada](https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/7/9/). This statement underscores the all-pervading nature of Krishna consciousness. It is not limited to any particular sphere or dimension; it is an active, living force that permeates every aspect of existence.

✨ Practical Application ✨

As we reflect on Verse 7.9, we are reminded of the all-encompassing presence of Lord Krishna in our lives. His fragrance, heat, life force, and guidance are ever-present, waiting to be acknowledged and surrendered to. Let us strive to cultivate Krishna consciousness in every sphere of our lives, recognizing His active presence in every moment, every breath, and every heartbeat.
